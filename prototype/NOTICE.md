# Public prototype notice

ICIP is an independent research prototype for responsible oncology real-world evidence, HEOR and evidence intelligence.

- The included NCRP/HEOR extracts are public or supplied research snapshots and must be checked against their cited sources before reuse.
- Vault-derived workflow language and synthetic examples are an adaptation for this prototype. This repository is not affiliated with or endorsed by any Vault Enterprise provider.
- No real patient records, protected health information, credentials, or private PDFs belong in this repository.
- The prototype is not a diagnostic, treatment, clinical-trial recruitment, regulatory or reimbursement decision system.
- Public APIs can change, rate-limit or become unavailable. Runtime status is labelled accordingly.

Before production use, complete DPDP/ethics review, data-sharing agreements, clinical validation, security review, licensing checks, and human approval controls.
